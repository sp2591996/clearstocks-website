About

Built by one person, for personal research

This site started as a personal project to systematically screen the Nifty 200 using a machine-learning model and basic valuation checks, instead of relying on gut feeling or scattered news. It's shared publicly so others can use the same tool — but it remains an independent, individually-run research project, not a financial firm or advisory service.

Accuracy

Every figure traces back to a real data source, and known limitations are documented rather than hidden.

Clarity

Scores and verdicts are explained in plain language, not left as unexplained numbers.

Independence

No paid placements, no sponsored rankings, no brokerage affiliation influencing any score.

Transparency

The methodology, its assumptions, and its failure modes are described openly in How It Works.
